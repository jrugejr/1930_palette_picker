About this prototype

This is a working proof of concept for a historical sign-painting palette explorer based on the digitized Phalen color-combination system.

The app flow is intentionally sign-painter-first:

  1. Choose the background or panel color.
  2. Choose the main copy color from real historical options.
  3. Browse complete historical palettes.
  4. View Ronan match and confidence notes.
  5. Track owned Ronan paints locally.

Ronan mappings and swatches are working notes and should be tested before any public official release.
