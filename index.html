About this prototype

This is a working proof of concept for a historical sign-painting palette explorer based on the digitized Phalen color-combination system.

The developing goal is to preserve the historical palette language while translating it into practical Ronan workflows: direct stock colors, Ronan formula colors, and makeable shop mixes.

  1. Choose the background or panel color.
  2. Choose the main copy color from real historical options.
  3. Browse complete historical palettes.
  4. View Ronan match and build notes.
  5. Track owned stock cans locally.

Ronan mappings, formulas, and swatches are working notes and should be tested before any public official release.
